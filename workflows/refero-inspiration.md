# Refero Inspiration Intake

## Purpose

Use Refero only during concept formation when a brief is visually or interactionally under-specified. Refero is a research input, not the final judge, a template licence, an asset source, or a new visual-source route.

The fixed authority order is:

```text
supplied reference or specification
> strongest accepted baseline
> explicit verbal brief and product truth
> Refero for named unresolved dimensions only
```

The route still remains `direct-build`, `reference-locked`, or `shared-canvas`. Refero may inform the lock; it never grants or replaces it.

## Choose the research lane

Refero exposes two different kinds of reference. Choose the one that matches the gap instead of treating everything as a style sample.

### 1. Product UI — screens and flows

Use [Refero](https://refero.design/search) when the unresolved question is functional or structural:

- page anatomy and information hierarchy;
- onboarding, checkout, account, settings, empty, error, permission, or cancellation flows;
- UI/UX patterns, state order, feedback, and interaction sequence;
- web-app versus iOS conventions;
- density and responsive clues visible in shipped product interfaces.

Extract the pattern and rationale. Do not reproduce a named product screen.

### 2. Refero Styles — visual-system language

Use [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/) when the unresolved question is art direction or design-system behaviour:

- palette roles and contrast;
- typography roles, scale, tracking, and line height;
- spacing, density, shape, border, elevation, and component weight;
- image, illustration, material, and motion direction;
- accessibility and do-not-use guidance.

A `DESIGN.md` is reusable system context, not a screenshot gallery. Adapt its reasoning into a short project-specific lock; do not paste a third party's complete file into the project.

Use both lanes only when both the product pattern and visual language are genuinely unresolved. Name one primary reference; a secondary reference may fill one named gap. Do not average several unrelated products into a visual collage.

## When to use it

Use a bounded Refero intake when:

- the input is a verbal brief with weak visual or UX specificity;
- the user explicitly asks for a premium, flagship, or public-quality direction;
- a new product needs a coherent page, flow, or design-system premise;
- a supplied image or accepted baseline leaves named UI, typography, spacing, or material gaps.

Skip it when:

- the visual system and journey are already locked;
- the task is a small correction;
- the product truth or accessibility need already decides the pattern;
- browsing would not change a concrete design decision.

## Access and terms boundary

Use Refero as a normal design-research service, within its current terms and plan limits.

- Do not scrape, crawl, harvest, bulk-download, mirror, or build a dataset from Refero pages or assets.
- Do not automate the public web UI. Agent-side automated discovery must use an official documented MCP/API under the applicable plan.
- Refero MCP currently requires a Pro subscription and sign-in. Do not install it, create an account, authorize it, upgrade, or pay without explicit user approval.
- Without approved official programmatic access, work from user-selected Refero URLs/screenshots or a human-led shortlist. A login wall or unavailable item is a stop condition, not permission to bypass access controls.
- Search-by-image can disclose the uploaded image to an external service. Never upload private, client, credential-bearing, unreleased, or confidential material without explicit approval.
- Catalogue size, pricing, free limits, and availability change. Do not hard-code them into project decisions.

Refero's terms permit design research, inspiration, comparison, education, and ordinary product-design use, but the displayed screenshots, interfaces, brands, fonts, and other materials may remain the property of Refero or third parties. A reference is not a reusable asset licence.

## Bounded research workflow

1. Name the unresolved decision: `flow`, `page anatomy`, `hierarchy`, `visual system`, `type`, `material`, or `motion`.
2. Choose `product-ui`, `styles`, or a justified `mixed` intake.
3. Make one focused query from product/audience, user job or page type, mood, theme, density, and one distinctive cue.
4. Shortlist no more than three candidates. Stop once they provide enough contrast to choose one direction.
5. Record only decision-ready observations.

For a Product UI candidate:

- source URL and product/page/flow name;
- user job and success state;
- page or flow anatomy;
- hierarchy and density;
- state, feedback, and interaction sequence;
- two useful rules and two patterns not to copy.

For a Styles candidate:

- source URL and style name;
- one-sentence visual premise;
- palette roles;
- typography behaviour and scale;
- spacing, density, shape, and elevation;
- material and motion principle;
- two useful rules and two patterns not to copy.

6. Reject any candidate that conflicts with product truth, supplied references, the accepted baseline, accessibility, performance, platform conventions, or rights.
7. Select one primary concept. If a secondary source is needed, state the single gap it fills.
8. Synthesize original project rules, tokens, components, copy, assets, and composition.
9. Write the project design lock before implementation. Do not browse Refero again during the same build/review cycle.

## Project design lock

```text
Research lane: product-ui | styles | mixed | none
Inspiration sources: <0-3 Refero URLs or user-supplied captures>
Primary source: <one source or none>
Secondary gap: <one named gap or none>
Authority order: <supplied reference | accepted baseline | verbal brief> > Refero
Premise: <what the visitor should feel and understand>
Journey/page anatomy: <major sections, states, or flow steps>
Palette roles: <canvas, surface, ink, muted, action, status>
Type: <display/body/mono roles, scale, tracking, line height>
Space and shape: <density, base unit, radius, border, elevation>
Image/material direction: <photographic, illustrated, 3D, procedural, none>
Motion principle: <job, tempo, restraint, reduced-motion alternative>
Do: <three concrete visible rules>
Avoid: <three concrete visible rules>
Originality boundary: <brand, layout, asset, font, or interaction details that must not be copied>
```

Once locked, implementation follows this record. A new reference, effect, library, or generated asset does not silently change it. Change the lock only when the user supplies a new direction or an objective blocker is recorded.

For image-led reconstruction, Refero may fill typography, UI chrome, spacing, or unnamed flow gaps. It cannot change the supplied composition, subject, silhouette, camera intent, named materials, or other visible invariants. For a verbal scene, it may make mood and interface behaviour concrete without pretending a source image exists.

## Rights and originality boundary

Do not copy or redistribute:

- brand names, logos, claims, product copy, or distinctive trade dress;
- screenshots, illustrations, photography, video, or downloadable assets;
- proprietary fonts without a verified project licence;
- a complete third-party `DESIGN.md`;
- an entire flow or distinctive layout so closely that the result becomes a disguised clone;
- Refero catalogue content as a public board, library, archive, dataset, or model-training source.

Keep source URLs and extracted principles in an internal research note. Public attribution or reuse is handled only when the underlying source licence requires and permits it.

## Relationship to the high-end review

Refero informs concept formation. It does not award points and does not replace `high-end-public-review.md`. The review judges how deliberately, accessibly, and originally the final artifact expresses its locked premise—not how closely it resembles a fashionable product.

Do not reopen Refero during the one-review protocol. If the concept itself is wrong, finish the scored review, stop, and ask which dimension missed. A fresh Refero search belongs to a user-authorized next iteration.
